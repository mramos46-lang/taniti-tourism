Travel FAQs

Practical information to help you prepare for your visit.

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and many accept major credit cards. Several banks facilitate currency exchange.

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Violent crime is very rare on Taniti. Reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased as tourism has grown.

Taniti has many national holidays. Many tourist attractions and restaurants close on holidays, so visitors should plan accordingly.

The drinking age on Taniti is 18.

Alcohol may not be served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m.
