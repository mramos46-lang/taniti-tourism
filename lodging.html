Regulated and Inspected Accommodations

All types of lodging on Taniti are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Planning Your Stay

Contact your preferred accommodation directly to confirm availability, rates, and reservations.

  1. Choose your travel dates and confirm how many people will be staying.
  2. Ask the property about available rooms and whether they can accommodate your whole party.
  3. Confirm the total price, cancellation terms, and reservation arrangements before booking.
