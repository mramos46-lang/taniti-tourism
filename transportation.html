Transportation

Plan how to reach Taniti and how you’ll explore once you arrive. Most visitors fly to the island, with ground transportation available for Taniti City and beyond.

Getting Here

Arrive by air

By Air

Almost all visitors arrive by air. Taniti’s small airport accommodates small jets and propeller planes. An airport expansion is planned to allow larger jets within the next few years.

Arrive by sea

By Cruise Ship

Some visitors arrive on a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week.

Getting Around

→

Rental Cars

Rental cars are available from a local rental agency near the airport.

↔

Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

◷

Public Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City every day from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.

⇢

Private Buses

Private buses serve the rest of the island beyond Taniti City.

◎

Bicycle Rentals

Bicycles and helmets can be rented from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

⌁

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.
